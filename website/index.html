End-to-End Cryptography

Every private message sent over Nearby Chat is cryptographically protected using 256-bit AES-GCM encryption. The message payload is encrypted directly on the sender's device and can only be decrypted by the final recipient.

Relay Protection: Intermediate nodes forwarding or routing your message only have access to the routing envelope headers (Source/Destination IDs, TTL). They cannot read, inject, or tamper with your private content.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does it work without cellular service?

Yes. Nearby Chat is completely serverless and requires absolutely no SIM card, cellular carrier signal, or mobile data subscription. It functions fully offline.

What is the maximum number of hops?

The mesh router caps the packet Time-to-Live (TTL) at a maximum of 8 hops to prevent loop overhead and optimize wireless channel bandwidth.

Is my location tracked?

No. Nearby Chat uses local network service discovery (mDNS) solely to identify local sockets. No location coordinates or telemetry are ever uploaded.

How do I secure my chats?

Tap the lock icon in the chat screen header to configure a shared password secret with your friend. All subsequent multi-hop messages will be securely encrypted using AES-GCM 256-bit.
